eko health

A CAD redesign of Eko's CORE 500 Stethoscope ear tip/ear stem to improve comfort and acoustic seal.

overview.

Role: Product Development Engineering Consultant

Duration: February 2025 - May 2025

Tools: SolidWorks, OnShape

Jump directly to final design

Background: This project was completed in Invention Corps, a human-centered design club at UC Berkeley. A group of 5, including myself, worked on improving the comfort and seal of Eko's CORE 500 Stethoscope ear tip/ear stem.

Problem: While researching current stethoscope ear tip designs for the Eko Health Core 500, we found that existing solutions present notable quality and functionality issues, including user discomfort over extended use, instability due to ear tips detaching too easily, and a lack of adaptability to diverse ear canal shapes and sizes. There is a gap between the ergonomic and performance needs of clinicians and how ear tip designs are currently engineered.

Feature Finalization

We decided to move forward with Design #10/#4 based on our CAD feature matrix, which used the same criteria as Phase 1.

Design #4 features the following:

  Ear Tip
  • Bulbous end
  • Outer ear contour alignment
  • Sleek, smooth exterior
  • Greater contact with ear
  • Two distinct contour regions
